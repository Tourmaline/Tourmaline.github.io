# Retrieval-practice question bank

A spaced-repetition study system for the blog posts. The goal: fix the
"can't recall it a month later" problem by replacing *re-reading* (recognition)
with *retrieval* (recall + re-derivation).

## How to use

1. Open a deck. For each card, **answer from a blank page before expanding the
   answer.** The effortful struggle is what builds memory — looking it up too
   early destroys the benefit.
2. Grade yourself honestly: ✅ got it / 🟡 partial / ❌ missed.
3. Update `review-log.md` with the date and your worst grade for the topic.
   Move the next-review date per the interval ladder below.

## The interval ladder (spaced repetition)

After a **successful** recall, stretch the next gap. After a miss, reset to the
start.

`1 day → 3 days → 1 week → 3 weeks → 2 months → 4 months`

A month-long first gap is why things were slipping — the first few reviews must
be close together.

## The 4-tier ladder (every topic)

- **T1 · Recall** — state a definition or result from memory.
- **T2 · Derive** — reproduce a key derivation on paper. *This is the real test
  for math.* Reciting that a result is true ≠ being able to produce it.
- **T3 · Apply** — a small twist on a concrete case.
- **T4 · Connect** — link to another topic or push beyond the post.

## Principles baked in

- **Interleave**: mix topics in a session rather than blocking one.
- **Elaborate**: say *why*, out loud, and connect to what you already know.
- **Re-derive, don't recite**: for anything with a `$$`, close the notes and
  rebuild the chain of equalities.
